# MAVEB Open Research Questions

Refresh: 2026-09-20.

1. What representation-stable notion of 'unchanged geometry' works for 3DGS when primitive count and indices change?
2. Does spectral/graph phase remain useful under anisotropic covariance, opacity variation and nonuniform sampling?
3. Can edit preservation yield a computable displacement bound for a useful restricted class of edits?
4. Can a heterogeneous dependency graph beat Gaussian-only local optimization across TSDF, mesh, Gaussian and renderer work?
5. Can an offline oracle prove adaptive mesh/Gaussian/TSDF assignment is worth implementing online?
6. What correspondence survives Gaussian split/merge/prune/densify?
7. How should unchanged-world damage separate photometric drift, surface drift, primitive drift and representation-equivalent change?
8. Can evidence age/support/contradiction improve deletion and reappearance beyond visibility confidence?
9. Where did old uncertainty mechanisms lack causal leverage, and can a new mechanism act on a decision that matters without reusing exposed confirmatory rooms?
10. Is there a unified-memory-specific algorithmic advantage after profiling, rather than merely a Metal implementation?
