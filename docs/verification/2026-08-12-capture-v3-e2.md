# Capture v3 durability and admission evidence — E2

Date: 2026-08-12

This record covers deterministic fixtures and a physical-device build/install check. It is E2
evidence, not a completed real-scene scan, interruption test, or 30-minute soak.

## Implemented

- The monotonic host timestamp is frozen in the `ARSessionDelegate` callback before main-actor and
  writer-queue latency.
- Active recordings append synchronized frame records to `frames.ndjson` and atomically replace a
  constant-size checkpoint instead of rewriting the growing canonical manifest per frame.
- Relaunch recovery ignores only a torn final record, validates frame order plus every committed
  plane path, size, and SHA-256, then compacts a canonical manifest.
- Frame admission is bounded to 15 Hz, responds to useful pose change, refreshes stationary views
  at 2 Hz, and rejects writer pressure, unusable depth, extreme clipping, and unusably soft images.
- CI has an independent signing-free `iPad Capture Compile` job.
